# Holm 2.2.3 — Burhou

Windows x64 MSIX version 2.2.3.0.

## Customer release notes

- Choose from 24 designs, including Prism Glass, Frutiger Aero, and Dot Matrix. Pick an accent colour or import your own design.
- Arrange the open notch in Settings › Start page. Reorder sections and tools, hide individual tools, and see changes in the live preview.
- Beam brings sharing into one tool. Send files to friends, nearby PCs, your phone, or the shelf. Scan a QR code to receive photos and files from your phone over Wi-Fi.
- An optional Holm account syncs settings, profile, friends, and achievements across computers. Chats, notes, reminders, and files stay on each device.
- Friend invites and chat use Holm's relay by default. Offline messages can wait up to a week. You can also remove friends from Chat.
- Mute notification pop-outs while keeping unread counts visible. The closed notch shows app icons beside the count. Dismissed Discord notifications stay dismissed after restarting.
- Show AI usage in the open notch while hiding the separate closed-notch rings.
- Holm uses less idle memory, animates more smoothly, and fits narrow or high-DPI displays. Light themes have clearer text and controls.
- Type Verity in Settings › General to find a little pet that dances to music.

## Validation

Verify Store-managed updates, Start with Windows after signing back in, and an upgrade from the live Store package before submission.
