Holm 1.9.1 brings Instagram-style reactions and GIFs to the chat, an equalizer that dances to your music, and keeps the weather visible during playback.

## Download

| File | For whom |
| --- | --- |
| **Holm-1.9.1-win-x64.zip** | Unzip and run `Holm.exe`; also used by Holm's automatic updater. |
| **Holm.exe** | Run the self-contained app directly; no .NET installation needed. |

Requires Windows 10 (2004 or newer) or Windows 11, x64.

Reactions and GIFs need your friends on Holm 1.9.1 too; on 1.9.0 a GIF shows as its link and reactions don't show.

Holm is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn on first launch. Click **More info → Run anyway** to launch it.

### Added
- **Reactions.** Hover a message and click the smiley for ❤️ 😂 😮 😢 😡 👍, or *+* for every emoji; double-click a message for a heart. You and your friend both see the reactions. React right from the notch when a message pops out, and change your quick reactions with *Customize reactions*.
- **GIFs.** *GIF* next to *Send*: search GIPHY or pick a recent GIF, or paste any GIF link. GIFs play in the chat, in the conversation list and in the notch. (Search needs a free GIPHY API key, pasted into the picker once.)
- **Equalizer that follows the music.** The bars in the closed notch now move with the beat of what plays. Only the loudness is read, nothing is recorded. *Settings › General* switches back to the classic bounce.
- **Weather during playback.** The open media notch keeps the weather icon and temperature at the top right. Hover for conditions and click anywhere in the weather area to change the city.

### Changed
- **Screen sharing.** The button in a chat now says *Share my screen*.
- **Holm icon.** The settings and friends sidebar uses the same white Holm icon as the app.

### Fixed
- **Text fields.** The cursor sits to the left of the placeholder in chat, profile and other Holm window inputs.
- **Changing the weather city.** During media playback, the small clock and weather stay visible while choosing a city, without a second large clock. The top toolbar stays centered.
