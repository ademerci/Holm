Holm 1.7.1 gives the right-click menu its size back: the on/off switches moved into a settings window of their own.

## Download

| File | For whom |
| --- | --- |
| **Holm-1.7.1-win-x64.zip** | Everyone. Unzip and run `Holm.exe`, nothing to install. |
| Holm-1.7.1-win-x64-dotnet10.exe | Smaller single exe if you already have the [.NET 10 Desktop Runtime](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet/10.0). |

Requires Windows 10 (2004 or newer) or Windows 11, x64.

> **“Windows protected your PC”?** The app is not code-signed, so SmartScreen warns on first launch. Click **More info → Run anyway**. Windows Firewall may also ask once – allow it if friends on your network should find you for Beam.

### Changed
- **Settings window.** The right-click menu had grown taller than the screen. Its on/off switches now live in a window of their own (right-click › *Settings…*), grouped and with real switches; Esc closes it. The menu keeps the timer, sleep timer, Beam text, receiving, friends and updates; messages, quiet hours, weather, the AI button and the sizes are under *Customize*.
