Holm 1.12.4 adds a pinnable, customizable large clock view in the open notch with a toggleable music bar at the bottom, and gives Bluetooth devices more breathing room.

## Download

| File | For whom |
| --- | --- |
| **Holm-1.12.4-win-x64.zip** | Unzip and run `Holm.exe`; also used by Holm's updater. |
| **Holm.exe** | Run the self-contained app directly; no .NET installation needed. |

Requires Windows 10 (2004 or newer) or Windows 11, x64.

Holm is not code-signed. Windows SmartScreen may warn on first launch; Smart App Control may block an unsigned app.

### Added
- **Large clock view in the open notch.** Clicking the clock in the open notch (on home or in the top bar while music plays) opens a large clock that fills the notch. Pin it open so it stays on screen, customize seconds, date, font style (*Light*, *Bold*, *Mono*) and colour, and control playback from the toggleable music bar at the bottom.

### Changed
- **More room for Bluetooth devices.** Battery chips in the open notch get vertical space between rows, and devices in the Bluetooth menu sit in taller rows.
